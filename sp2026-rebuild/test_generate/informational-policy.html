Guiding principles

University technology resources support teaching, research, and administration. Access is provided in service of the academic community and depends on responsible, lawful use.

Users should respect the rights, privacy, and work of others while helping maintain an open and secure environment.

Policy scope

This policy applies to university-managed devices, software, networks, accounts, and data. Users remain responsible for protecting credentials and following applicable security requirements wherever they access University resources.

Local procedures may add requirements for a particular service or unit, provided they are consistent with University policy.

Prohibited conduct

Examples of conduct that is not permitted include:

  • Accessing another person's account or files without authorization.
  • Disrupting the operation or availability of shared systems.
  • Sharing credentials or exposing confidential information.
  • Using software or media in violation of its license or applicable law.

Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected misuse to the appropriate computing unit or University security contact. Violations may result in access restrictions, disciplinary action, or referral to appropriate authorities.

For Smith IT assistance, contact helpme@rhsmith.umd.edu.

Smith School addendum

Data protection

Store and share institutional data only through approved services and follow the data owner's access requirements.

Service access

Smith IT may access a supported device as needed to provide maintenance, security updates, and service.
