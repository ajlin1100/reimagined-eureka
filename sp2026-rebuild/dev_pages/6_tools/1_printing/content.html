🖨︎ Student & Visitor Printing (College Park)

The Office of Smith IT partners with WEPA Printing Services to provide cloud-based self-service kiosks. WEPA kiosks stock high-quality paper and offer both high-speed black & white and full-color printing.

Payment & Discount Rates

Printing rates vary by color mode. Duplex (two-sided) printing is available at a discounted rate to help maximize value and reduce paper waste.

Shared Printer Courtesy Guidelines

🕒︎ Large Jobs: Reserve extensive print runs (50+ pages) for off-peak or less busy hours.
♻︎ Sustainability: Promptly discard unwanted or test pages into provided recycling bins.

👩︎‍🏫︎ Faculty & Staff Printing (College Park)

Faculty and staff are automatically granted access to designated departmental printers based on office location through Active Directory on UMD-owned computers.

Technical Support & Guest Access

• Access Assistance: Current faculty/staff needing printer mapping help should contact the IT Service Desk.
• Guest Access Requests: Non-staff requiring temporary department printer access must have an authorized faculty or staff representative request access on their behalf.
