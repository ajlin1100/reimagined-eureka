After-Hours Network or Server Outages

Enterprise Infrastructure, Applications, and Security (EIAS) maintains a 24/7 Enterprise Infrastructure Emergency Response Team. Automated monitoring tools check systems around the clock to alert on-call staff of critical service failures.

While most issues are detected and addressed proactively before impacting users, please report any suspected critical system outage immediately.

Our Mission

The Office of Smith IT provides excellent customer service in support of the unique computing needs of the Robert H. Smith School community (students, alumni, faculty, staff, and visitors). We accomplish this by:

  • Cultivating an adaptable IT environment that embraces evolving technologies
  • Building a world-class, robust, secure, and heterogeneous infrastructure
  • Delivering technology that is innovative, convenient, optimized, secure, and always available
  • Supporting and strengthening the strategic pillars of the Smith School through state-of-the-art technology
