Mobile Devices

Mobile device procurement, eligibility requirements, and setup services for Business School faculty and staff through Verizon Wireless.

Policy Notice: The Business School maintains a mobile contract with Verizon Wireless. Eligible employees may receive approval for one line of service paid for by the Business School upon meeting eligibility guidelines.

📋︎ Requesting a Mobile Phone

Mobile phone requests must be placed by an Academic Department Chair or Department Supervisor on behalf of an eligible employee. All submissions undergo review and approval by the Office of the Dean.

1

Submit Request

Supervisor or Chair completes the official request form for the employee.

📱︎ Mobile Request Form
2

Dean's Review

The Office of the Dean reviews and approves the eligibility and mobile line allocation.

3

IT Activation & Delivery

Devices are delivered to Smith IT, where our team activates the phone and configures email, calendar, and contact sync.
