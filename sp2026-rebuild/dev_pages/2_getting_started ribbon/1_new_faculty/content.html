Getting Started

For New Faculty

Welcome to the Robert H. Smith School of Business!

1 Before You Arrive

Part-Time Faculty

Department administrators should contact Smith IT directly to ensure part-time faculty are appropriately equipped prior to their start date.

Submit a Ticket

Full-Time Faculty

New full-time faculty receive a standard laptop or desktop from Smith IT. Submit your equipment choices via the online request portal.

Submit Tech Request

Additional Hardware & Software: Equipment beyond standard options can be purchased using departmental or personal funds. If you require specialized software outside our standard software image, please note it in your request form so we can assist in identifying funding options.

2 When You Arrive

🔑︎

Set Up Your Campus Directory ID

Your Directory ID grants access to essential systems including Canvas (ELMS), campus Wi-Fi, and lab/classroom computers. Note: Account setup is available as soon as HR/Payroll activates your appointment.

Activate Directory ID
💻︎

Schedule an IT Orientation

Sit down with a Smith IT team member for a 20-minute orientation to walk through our systems and ensure all your access points are configured properly.

📚︎

Teaching Preparation & Software

Course Setup: Log into Canvas (ELMS) using your Directory ID to prepare course sites. For Canvas support, email canvas@rhsmith.umd.edu.

Lab & Classroom Software: Need specific software installed for your courses? Submit a request as early as possible (standard collection periods occur in January and May).

Request Classroom Software
🎙︎

Classroom & AV Walkthrough

Schedule a hands-on introduction to the AV equipment in your assigned classrooms and conference rooms with the Smith IT AV Unit.

Contact AV Support: helpme@rhsmith.umd.edu | 301-405-5213

🔬︎

Connect with your Departmental Technology Officer (DTO)

If your work involves research computing or specialized departmental labs, connect with your DTO to discuss infrastructure and computing resources.

🏢︎

Verify Office Setup

Inspect the hardware and network setup in your assigned office. If anything needs adjustment or troubleshooting, visit us in person at 3520 Van Munching Hall or contact the Service Desk.

3 Before Departing

When concluding your appointment with the School of Business, please schedule an exit interview with HR. In addition to returning physical keys and your campus ID, you must return all official IT hardware:

University & Grant-Funded Equipment: All laptops, mobile devices, monitors, and peripherals purchased using University funds, departmental accounts, ceiling funds or grants must be returned prior to your departure date.

For questions regarding the HR exit process, please contact Bobvita Salters at x59571 or via email at bsjones@umd.edu.
