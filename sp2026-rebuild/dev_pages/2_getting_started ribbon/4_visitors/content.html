Individual Guest Access

Visitors to the University of Maryland campus can connect directly to the guest Wi-Fi network for self-service internet access on personal devices.

Bulk / Event Group Access

Sponsoring a conference or group visit? Event organizers can request temporary bulk wireless credentials for guests through Smith IT.

Submit GWA Request

Advance Notice Required: Bulk Guest Wireless Access (GWA) requests must be submitted at least one week prior to your visitors' scheduled arrival on campus.

For additional information on campus network coverage, eduroam, and wireless policies, visit the Division of Information Technology's UMD Wireless Portal.
