1 Student Account Provisioning

Welcome to the Robert H. Smith School of Business! Upon acceptance into a Smith Graduate Program, your campus IT accounts are automatically scheduled for creation based on student rosters provided by the Masters Programs Office.

📬︎

Receiving Your Account Details

Your official account credentials and setup instructions will be mailed to you prior to the start of the semester.

Missing Account Credentials? If you have not received your IT account information prior to the start of the semester, please contact the Masters Programs Office directly at smithmasters@umd.edu.
