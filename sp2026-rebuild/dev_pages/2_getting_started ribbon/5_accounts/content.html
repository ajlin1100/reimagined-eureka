Campus Directory Accounts & Email

Authentication, storage, and communication services

1 Campus Directory ID Overview

Your Directory ID is the primary login used to access essential University systems, including Canvas (ELMS), UMD Email, campus Wi-Fi, and Business School network resources (such as classroom/lab PCs and shared network drives).

🔑︎

Payroll-Activated Setup

Account setup becomes available as soon as HR/Payroll activates your appointment in the system. If you encounter setup errors, please verify your appointment status with HR.

Activate Campus Directory ID

Not on UMD Payroll? Individuals not processed through campus payroll do not receive automatic directory accounts. If access is needed, your host department can sponsor an Affiliate Account or an Associate Account on your behalf.

Provisioning & Departure Policy

File storage and email are generated automatically after HR confirms payroll appointments. Faculty and staff accounts remain active until the final day of employment. Be sure to copy or remove personal files before your end date, as access ends upon departure.

3 Email Services by Role

👔︎

Faculty & Staff

Email accounts are created automatically by the Division of IT (DIT). Review technical specifications and client setup guides in the DIT Knowledge Repository.

🎓︎

MBA & Specialty Masters Students

Official communication is sent to your <directoryid>@umd.edu account hosted on UMD G Suite (Google Workspace for Education). Learn more at go.umd.edu/umdgsuite.

Graduate students also receive a secondary Terpmail account (<directoryid>@terpmail.umd.edu). Approximately two semesters after graduation, official @umd.edu mail forwards to Terpmail for post-graduation use. Find details at go.umd.edu/umdterpmail.

🎒︎

Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students receive a Terpmail account on the Google Workspace platform for all university communication.

Note on Legacy Email: Since Spring 2022, all graduate students use the central UMD G Suite account (@umd.edu). Legacy options such as Smoogle or @rhsmith email accounts have been decommissioned.
