2 Before Departing

When concluding your employment at the Robert H. Smith School of Business, please schedule an exit interview with the HR office. Ensure you return all university property, including:

  • Office keys
  • Campus ID Card
  • All IT equipment purchased with any University, departmental, or grant funds (laptops, mobile phones, tablets, monitors, peripherals, etc.)

University Asset Regulations: Talk to your Business Office representative for full details regarding State regulations and ownership compliance for computer equipment.

Be sure to finalize any pending leave payback, loans, and leave settlements at this time. For exit interview inquiries, contact Bobvita Salters at x59571 or via email at bsjones@umd.edu.
