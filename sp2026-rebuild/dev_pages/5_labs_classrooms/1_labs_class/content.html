Labs & Classrooms

The Robert H. Smith School of Business provides technology-enhanced classrooms and learning labs across its locations.

🎥︎ Technology-Enhanced Classroom Features

Every satellite and main campus classroom is outfitted with advanced instructional technology designed to enrich both in-person and hybrid learning environments:

🎙︎ Lecture Capture & Streaming

High-definition video cameras paired with ceiling microphone arrays and ceiling speakers enable seamless lecture recording, live streaming, and on-demand review via Panopto.

🖊︎ Interactive Smart Boards

Collaborative whiteboard technology allows instructors and students to annotate simultaneously, capture ideas in real-time, and save digital notes directly from projected displays.

📱︎ Wireless Presentation & AirMedia

Connect wirelessly using iOS, Android, macOS, or Windows devices. Effortlessly present slides, spreadsheets, PDFs, and media using AirMedia screen sharing.

🛠︎ Instructor & Software Support

Need Software or AV Support in Your Classroom?

To ensure required software is pre-installed on classroom PCs before the semester begins, instructors should submit a Software Request. Software published through vSmith is accessible anytime from any location.
