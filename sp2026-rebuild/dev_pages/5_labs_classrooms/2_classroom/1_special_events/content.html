Special Events & Departmental AV Support

Smith IT provides specialized staffing, custom equipment staging, and technical installation services for events, conferences, and departmental facility upgrades.

Smith IT staff support standard institutional functions including academic lectures, visual presentations, professional conferences, and general business meetings.

🔑︎ Mandatory Requirements for Event Organizers

  • Training & Orientation: Any group planning to operate facility AV equipment must schedule mandatory prior instruction and orientation with Audio Visual Services.
  • Non-Smith School Groups: Any external or non-Smith School organization seeking to utilize technology in Van Munching Hall spaces must obtain prior authorization from Smith IT Management.

📋︎ Requesting Support & Operational Rules

Special events place heightened operational demands on support personnel. All event assistance is subject to service fees determined by Audio Visual Services Management.

📌︎ Room Booking & Staffing Guidelines

  • Schedule Room First: Secure your venue through Smith Operations (301-405-2189) prior to submitting AV requests. Always include setup and teardown buffers in your reservation time.
  • Support Level Determination: Required technician staffing levels and equipment allocations are evaluated and assigned directly by Smith IT Management.

⏰︎ Weekend & Overtime Premium Rates

Saturday & Sunday Events: Weekend bookings require a 4-hour (half-day) minimum charge, plus a 50% surcharge for work outside standard operating hours.
Overtime Rates: Any support duration exceeding 8 hours in a single day is billed at double the standard hourly rate.

🛠︎ Departmental Equipment Installation Services

The AV Services team performs professional installation and configuration of hardware inside departmental spaces. All hardware must be formally recommended by Smith IT and approved by the AV Manager prior to purchase; non-endorsed equipment cannot be installed or serviced.

⚠︎ Scope of Work & Construction Limitations

Installation billing covers technical mounting, hardware connections, and software configuration only. It does not include electrical wiring, data port drops, or structural facility modifications. Estimates provided by the AV Manager are preliminary and subject to final adjustments.

Support resources, technician availability, and required hours are determined by Smith IT. Service is not guaranteed until you receive formal confirmation and accept the provided cost estimate.
