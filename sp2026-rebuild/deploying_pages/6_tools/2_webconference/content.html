📋︎ Best Practices for Web Meetings

Follow these key recommendations to ensure seamless, professional, and disruption-free online sessions across any platform:

🎙︎ Audio & Mute Management

Ask participants to mute microphones when not speaking (or mute all upon entry) to prevent background noise. Utilize hand-raising features to moderate speakers.

🎧︎ Hardware & Echo Reduction

Use a dedicated headset with an integrated microphone rather than built-in speakers to minimize feedback and echo.

🖥︎ Screen Sharing Preparation

Open PowerPoint presentations and files prior to start. Clear sensitive or unnecessary clutter from your desktop screen before sharing.

⚙︎ Advanced Setup & Rehearsal

Verify meeting settings in advance. Rehearse controls with a colleague beforehand to familiarize yourself with host privileges and screen sharing.

👨︎‍🏫︎ Guest Speaker Dry Runs

Conduct a practice run with guest speakers a few days prior, ensuring they connect using the exact device and location planned for the live event.

💬︎ Large Group Moderation

Designate a co-host or moderator for large audiences to monitor incoming chat questions and facilitate Q&A flow.

🔧︎ Quick Troubleshooting Tip

If a host or participant experiences connection or media issues during a meeting, the quickest fix is usually to try a different or fully updated web browser. If technical issues persist, contact the platform's designated support line.
