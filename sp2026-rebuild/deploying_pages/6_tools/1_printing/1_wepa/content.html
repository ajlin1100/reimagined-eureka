WEPA Printing Instructions

WEPA (Wireless Everywhere Print Anywhere) allows you to print seamlessly from your personal computer, mobile device, cloud accounts, or a USB drive to any kiosk on campus.

Important Notice for Students: Do NOT create a new account with WEPA. An account has automatically been created for you and linked to your Directory ID. After logging in to a kiosk once with your campus credentials, you will be able to swipe your UMD ID card to log in automatically.

How to Print

1From Your Computer (Personal Laptop/Desktop)

  1. Download the WEPA Driver
  2. Open your document and print, selecting either WEPA-BW or WEPA-COLOR as your printer.
  3. Log in with your UMD Directory ID and password when prompted.
  4. Go to any WEPA Kiosk and swipe your campus ID card to release your job.*

2Contactless & Mobile Printing (WEPA Express)

  1. Download the WEPA Mobile App or Desktop Application via the WEPA Express Page.
  2. Log in using your UMD Directory ID and password.
  3. Print directly from cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) or your local device to kiosks located in Van Munching Hall or the Ronald Reagan Building without touching the kiosk screen.

3Direct from a USB Drive

  1. Go to any WEPA kiosk and tap Print from USB on the main screen.
  2. Insert your USB drive into the kiosk USB slot.
  3. Select your document to print. (Supported formats: PDF or Microsoft Office files like .doc, .xls, .ppt).

4From Campus Computer Labs

  1. Open your document, click File, then select Print.
  2. Choose WEPA-BW or WEPA-COLOR from the printer list.
  3. Log in with your UMD Directory ID and password when prompted.
  4. Head to any WEPA kiosk and swipe your campus ID to release your print job.*

*First-time users must log in once at the physical kiosk using their Directory ID credentials to link their physical campus ID card for future swipe access.
