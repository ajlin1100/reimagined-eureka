🚀︎ Getting Started

How do I get started with vSmith Applications & Desktops?

Connecting via the Omnissa Horizon Client offers full functionality, including local file access, USB redirection, and local printing capabilities.

  1. Download and install the latest version of the Horizon Client from VMware, accepting all default installation options.
  2. When prompted for a server address, enter:
desktop.rhsmith.umd.edu
  1. Open the Omnissa Horizon Client from your Start menu (Windows) or Finder (Mac).
  2. Double-click the server listed and log in with your University Directory ID and password.
  3. Double-click any entitled application or desktop to launch.

🔒︎ Access & Entitlements

Why can't I see or access vSmith Applications or Desktops?

vSmith resources are specifically provisioned for Smith School of Business courses. If you have not been entitled to any desktops or apps, nothing will display upon login.

If you are studying at the Smith School and believe you require access, please email the Office of Smith IT Service Desk at helpme@rhsmith.umd.edu with the following details:

  • Your Full Name
  • Your official @umd.edu email address
  • The Course Number requiring vSmith access
  • The name of your Course Professor
  • The specific Software Application you need to access

⏱︎ Requests are processed by vSmith administrators, who will investigate and contact you within two business days with the access decision.

💾︎ Saving Your Work & File Safety

Because vSmith operates on temporary virtual machines, saving your work correctly is vital to prevent data loss.

Recommended Storage: TerpDrive

We recommend using TerpDrive as your default saving method while using vSmith.

Working with Data ↗

⚠︎ Critical Notice: Unsaved Work is Permanently Lost

When you connect to a vSmith Application or Desktop, you are assigned a temporary machine out of a large shared pool. Any files stored locally without saving to a network drive, cloud service (such as Google Drive), or USB thumb drive will be permanently destroyed when you log off.

🛑︎ 15-Minute Disconnect Timeout: If you disconnect from your vSmith session without logging off, you will automatically be logged off in 15 minutes, and unsaved work will be lost.

🖨︎ Printing & Technical Troubleshooting

Printing from vSmith

• WEPA Printing: Students can print to the WEPA service and collect print jobs at their convenience.
• Local Printers: Any local printers installed on your host device (the computer connecting to vSmith) are also available to print to.

macOS Full-Screen Freeze Fix

If you run vSmith full screen on a Mac and the screen freezes, exit out of full screen mode to resume working. You can resize the screen without issue, but returning to full screen mode will cause it to freeze again.
