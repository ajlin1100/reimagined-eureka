Faculty Parking Sticker Requests

Faculty teaching at the DC campus can request parking validation stickers by following these steps:

Green Sticker ($14.00)
After 5:00 PM / Weekends / Holidays
Pink Sticker ($22.00)
All Day Access
Grey Sticker ($19.00)
4 Hours or Less
Red Sticker ($17.00)
2 Hours or Less
Yellow Sticker ($10.00)
1 Hour or Less
  1. Submit request via email to smith_operations@rhsmith.umd.edu.
  2. Specify the sticker type(s) and quantity needed.
  3. Provide your department spending account number for reimbursement.
  4. Notify your department accountant regarding the chargeback.

💻︎ Computer Labs & Workstations

The DC Suite contains 6 BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) workstations and 2 high-capacity printers.

Each workstation provides a high-definition monitor and an HDMI cable connection. Users with devices lacking native HDMI ports should bring an appropriate adapter. Contact Smith IT or consult your device specs for compatibility advice.
