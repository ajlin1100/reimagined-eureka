🏛︎ Classrooms & Learning Spaces

Van Munching Hall houses a comprehensive suite of instructional spaces designed for interactive lectures, team discussions, and specialized software instruction:

Standard Classrooms & Seminar Rooms

Includes 23 standard classrooms (seating 24 to 65 students) and 2 PhD seminar rooms. Auditoriums accommodate larger lectures of up to 250 students.

Computer Classrooms

Equipped with dedicated workstations for hands-on technical instruction:

• VMH 2203 - IBM TQ Theatre
• VMH 3505 - Financial Markets Teaching Theatre

• VMH 3522 - Supply Chain Lab

Classroom & Event Reservations

Classrooms are primarily scheduled for academic coursework. To inquire about booking a classroom or auditorium for a special event, contact the Scheduling Office.

Email Scheduling Office (scheduling@rhsmith.umd.edu) ↗

🖥︎ Open Computer Labs & Virtual Access

Three open computer labs are available during active class hours to anyone holding a valid University Directory ID. Workstations come pre-configured with software required for BMGT coursework.

Remote Access via vSmith

If specific course software is unavailable in the physical labs due to seating or licensing limits, BMGT students can access required software remotely via the virtual environment.


🔬︎ Research & Specialized Teaching Labs

Van Munching Hall features specialized, discipline-specific facilities equipped with advanced hardware and analytical software:

VMH 3505

Financial Markets Teaching Theatre

Equipped with professional financial data terminals and tools including Bloomberg, Thomson Reuters Eikon, and Capital IQ.

VMH 3518

Behavioral Laboratory

Configured for empirical research using advanced tools such as MediaLab, DirectRT, and Eye Tracking systems.

VMH 3522

Supply Chain Lab

Dedicated environment for supply chain management, modeling, and logistics simulations.
