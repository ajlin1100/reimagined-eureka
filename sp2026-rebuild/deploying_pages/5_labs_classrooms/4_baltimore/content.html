📶︎ Wireless Network Access

Two secure wireless networks are available throughout the Baltimore Peninsula suite:

eduroam (Primary Network)

High-speed secure connection for UMD students, faculty, and staff. Authenticate using your full @umd.edu email address and Directory password.

Download WiFi Auto-Config App ↗

UM Conf (Guest Network)

Temporary wireless access for campus visitors and guests. Setup instructions and daily connection codes are available on pamphlets posted throughout the suite.

For troubleshooting wireless connectivity issues, contact DivIT support at (301) 405-1500 or see your on-site IT support coordinator.
