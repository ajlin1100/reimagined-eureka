Classroom Support Services

The Smith IT Audiovisual Unit provides technical management, instructional media support, live session assistance, and equipment reservations for classrooms and learning spaces.

📽︎ Supported Audio/Visual Classroom Services

Our staff assists faculty and guest speakers with multimedia integration, digital presentations, and hybrid learning setups across multiple formats:

📹︎ Lecture Capture (Panopto)

Automated or manual recording of classroom lectures, presentation slides, and audio feeds for student review.

💻︎ Virtual Meetings & Hybrid

Integrated web conferencing setup supporting live remote guest speakers and dual-delivery instruction via Zoom or WebEx.

🎙︎ Events & Panel Discussions

Support for multi-microphone audio amplification, wireless lapels, audience Q&A mics, and high-definition video broadcasting.

📋︎ Requesting AV Services & Equipment

Advance Request Policy (7-Day Notice Required)

All equipment reservations, media requests, and scheduled in-classroom support must be submitted online at least 7 business days prior to your planned session or event.

Important: Submitting an AV request does not reserve the physical room. You must secure a formal space reservation with the Scheduling Department before AV services can be confirmed.

Late Requests (< 7 Days): If submitting a request with less than 7 days' notice, fill out the online form and immediately contact the AV Manager at x59548 or Master Control at x55213 to confirm technician availability.

🏛︎ Support for Special Events & Non-Standard Demands

Technical support requested outside standard operating hours or events that create unusually high staffing demands on the AV team require prior approval from the Audio Visual Manager.

Note: Special events requiring dedicated AV personnel or custom setups are subject to service fees determined by Audio Visual Services Management.
