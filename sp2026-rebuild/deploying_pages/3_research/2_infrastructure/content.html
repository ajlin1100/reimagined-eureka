Cloud virtual machines for hosting applications and running research workloads. UMD's negotiated contract reduces computing costs and prevents work product siloing. Storage & Backup Solutions

  • Google Cloud Storage: Cloud storage integrated with UMD Workspace accounts Moderate Risk Data
  • UMD Box: Campus-provided cloud storage starting at 25GB High Risk Data
  • Networked Storage: Multi-protocol data storage hosted by DivIT for research units.
  • Data Backups: Local UMD data backup running IBM Spectrum Protect.

2 Usage Policies & Data Classification

Software Licensing: All UMD and Smith School software platforms are licensed strictly for academic, educational, and research use.

🛡︎

Data Classification Standards

Faculty and staff must adhere to UMD data protection regulations when storing research data:

• Google Drive: Approved for data categorized up to Moderate Risk.
• UMD Box: Approved for data categorized up to High Risk.

Review the IT-2 Data Classification Standard, review the UMD Acceptable Use Policy, or contact IT Compliance with questions.
