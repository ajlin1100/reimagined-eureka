Meet the Research Support Team

Cross-functional IT consulting and solutions for Business School research initiatives

Our Research Support Team employs a cross-functional approach, ensuring comprehensive solutions by harnessing the diverse skills of our members. We assist with every aspect of your research project, from gathering requirements and consulting on grant proposals to defining technology solutions for both local and cloud services. We also capture specific data and provide solutions for easier analysis, addressing any area needed for your project's success.

When specialized skills outside our expertise are required, we collaborate with external colleagues. If you don't know someone who can help get your project off the ground, we do!
