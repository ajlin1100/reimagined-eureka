🔄︎

Provisioning & Refresh Guidelines

Replacement Hires: Replacement staff and faculty utilize the equipment previously assigned to that position or employee.
Refresh Cycle: Smith IT no longer automatically replaces computers every 3–4 years. System refreshes are executed as departmental resources allow, and apply only to primary PCs (computers purchased by individuals or departments directly are not refreshed).

2 Purchasing Technology

🏢︎

Purchases Through Smith IT (Asset-Tagged)

All technology requiring an asset tag must be purchased through the Office of Smith IT to maintain compliant inventory records for university auditing. Equipment is delivered directly to Smith IT (3520 Van Munching Hall) to be tagged, logged, and scheduled for installation/configuration.

🖱︎

Purchases Through Department Coordinators (Peripherals)

Minor accessories and peripherals under $150 (keyboards, mice, USB drives, external hard drives, toner, etc.) should be ordered through department coordinators via a designated University credit card. If you are unsure whether an item requires an asset tag, verify with Smith IT prior to purchase.

Terms & Conditions (No Returns Policy): Under the terms and conditions of Smith IT and our vendors, it is understood and acknowledged that no returns will be accepted after purchase. All returns of any system, monitor, and/or peripheral will be due to hardware or manufacturer defect and the following actions will be taken by Smith IT:

  1. The defective part will be repaired/replaced by vendor warranty.
  2. If the defective item is unable to be repaired, it will be RMA'ed (Return Merchandise Authorization) and a replacement of the defective item will be received. The replacement item will be the same item that was originally requested for purchase.

NOTE: The Office of Smith IT does not refresh any computer purchased by individuals or departments. Only primary PCs are put on the 3–4 year refresh cycle.

3 Computer Support Scope

🛠︎

Supported vs. Non-Standard Systems

Standard Systems: Full support is provided for all standard Dell laptops and desktops.
Non-Standard & Custom Systems: Custom builds and Macintosh computers are classified as Non-Standard. Support for non-standard systems is provided on a best-effort basis and is limited to deploying licensed software onto the pre-installed OS. Smith IT cannot rebuild custom operating systems, and hardware repairs remain the owner's responsibility (as enterprise agreements are tied to Dell).

⚙︎

Component Upgrades & Personal Devices

Smith IT assists with installing replacement components on supported systems. Note that adding third-party hardware may change a system's classification from supported to custom. The Office of Smith IT does not support and cannot be held liable for issues that occur on personally-owned devices.
