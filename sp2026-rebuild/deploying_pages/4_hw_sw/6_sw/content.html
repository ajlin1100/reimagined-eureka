🛒︎ Purchasing Software

You can purchase software with negotiated UMD discounts through the Division of IT software licensing site or directly from vendors.

If you are using an RHS Spending Account, the Office of Finance and Administration can assist with placing the order. Please contact the manager responsible for your spending account before purchasing.

🛒︎ DIT Software Licensing Website
⚠︎ Important Policy Note on Reimbursement:
While you may install non-standard software on your PC, the Office of Administration advises that non-supported software will not be reimbursed, nor can spending ceiling funds be used for non-standard software purchases. Contact your account Business Manager for questions regarding this policy.
