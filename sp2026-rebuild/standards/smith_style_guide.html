Imagery

Images should convey energy and personality to reflect the bold and strong tone of our voice. If you are providing images, be mindful that images you provide should be of high resolution (300 DPI) and as large as possible. We can always scale and crop down, but we can’t size an image up and retain its quality.

You can also browse through our digital asset library for any images you need. Additionally, stock imagery is available online at subscription-based sites like Adobe Stock, iStock and Getty Images, or at free sites like Unsplash and Pexel.
